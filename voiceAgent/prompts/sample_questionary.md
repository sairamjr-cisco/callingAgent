### 2. Standard Agent Questions for Denial Management

#### Questions for CPT Denial Codes & Reasons
* *"Can you tell me line-by-line which specific CPT/HCPCS codes were denied, and what exact denial reason code or remark code is associated with each line?"*
* *"For the code denied as experimental/investigational, is there a formal medical necessity appeal process, or can clinical notes and medical records be submitted for reconsideration?"*
* *"For the service denied as incidental, is a modifier required (such as modifier 25 or 59/X-modifiers), or can this line be reconsidered if billed separately?"*
* *"What specific supporting documentation was missing for this line (e.g., operative notes, invoice, medical records), and where should it be submitted?"*

#### Questions for Document / Record Request Denials
* *"What specific medical records are needed to reprocess this claim (e.g., history & physical, progress notes, procedure/test results)?"*
* *"Can these records be uploaded via the provider portal, or do they need to be mailed/faxed? What is the timely filing deadline to submit these records?"*
* *"What is the exact mailing address, department, or fax number we should use when sending in the requested records?"*

#### General Follow-Up Questions for Denied Claims
* *"Is there an appeal option available for this denial, and what is the exact deadline (e.g., 60 days vs. 365 days from denial date) to file?"*
* *"Does this require a formal written appeal, or can it be reopened/reprocessed over the phone or via a corrected claim once records/modifiers are submitted?"*
* *"Can I please get your name and the call reference number for today’s inquiry?"*
